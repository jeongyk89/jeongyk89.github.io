About OKLab / OKLCH. Palettes here were selected in sRGB and expressed as hex. The tool converts each color to OKLCH on the fly so you can judge perceived lightness (L) and chroma (C). If you prefer OKLCH-first design, you can still use these as anchors and adjust L/C/H consistently across a palette.

Contrast badges show WCAG 2.1 contrast ratios of each swatch against the current background and against black/white body text. Aim for ≥ 4.5:1 for normal text.
