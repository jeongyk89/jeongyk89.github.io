About

I'm an Associate Professor in Sustainable Production Logistics at KTH Royal Institute of Technology in Stockholm (since 2026). My research connects simulation, digital twins, and AI with the practical decisions people make in production and logistics.

I previously worked at KTH as an Assistant Professor (2021–2026) and postdoctoral researcher (2019–2021). Before joining KTH, I was a postdoctoral researcher at Seoul National University, where I also earned my PhD (2018) and BSc (2011) in Naval Architecture and Ocean Engineering.

Research

Smart production logistics & digital transformation
How material and information flows are planned, monitored, and managed as production digitalises.
Digital twins & simulation for decision support
Simulation and data-connected models for exploring alternatives, testing assumptions, and supporting engineering decisions, including interaction through large language models.
Machine learning & computer vision for human-centred production
Interpreting operator activity and work context to support order picking, manual assembly, and material handling.
Sustainable & circular manufacturing
Simulation and decision tools for circular and sustainable operations, from remanufacturing to urban mobility.

Teaching

At KTH, I am course coordinator and lecturer for Modelling, Simulation and Optimization of Sustainable Production (ML2302) and Digitalisation for Sustainable Production (ML2303) in the Master's Programme in Sustainable Production Development. I am also examiner for Production Logistics and Supply Chains (ML2305) and contribute to teaching in Theory of Science and Research Methodology in Sustainable Production Development (ML2307).

My teaching combines structured preparation, hands-on work, and industrial cases. I aim to help students ask useful questions, test assumptions, and explain their own engineering decisions, including when using AI. I also supervise doctoral and master's students and contribute to first-cycle and professional education.
