Human–Robot Collaborative Order Picking

Sequence diagram synthesized from the 15-stage workflow. It shows the main information exchanges among WMS, robot control, human picker, AMR/cart, layout/navigation services, and sensing/validation technologies.

Static branches: rule-based options such as FIFO, fixed allocation, paper/digital display.
Dynamic branches: adaptive decisions based on congestion, availability, fatigue, and real-time state.
Human factors: fatigue, stress, cognitive load, autonomy, job satisfaction, ergonomics.
