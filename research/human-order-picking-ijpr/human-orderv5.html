Remanufacturing Warehouse Order Picking – Human & AI Interaction

Focus: Remanufacturing warehouses face mixed-material bins, variable part conditions, and complex verification. Below are the as‑is process and an AI‑enhanced view. AI is grouped into: LLM Help, Vision AI, and Logistics Robot.
