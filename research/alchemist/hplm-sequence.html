Cyber-Ethical Blueprint for Trustworthy AI in Logistics

1. Ethics-by-Design Lifecycle (Methodology Pipeline)

flowchart TD A([Start: Project Kickoff]) --> B[Ethical Risk Assessment (Requirements Stage)] B --> C[Map Risks -> Controls (Principles -> Design Controls)] C --> D[Standards Mapping (EU AI Act / GDPR / IEC 62443 / ISO 23247 / IEEE 7000)] D --> E[Embed Controls in Architecture (Human Override, Logging, Consent, Safety)] E --> F[Validation and Compliance Testing (Simulations, Audits, Checklists)] F --> G{Meets Ethical Criteria} G -- No --> B G -- Yes --> H[Release Gate: Ethics and Security Sign-off] H --> I[Continuous Monitoring (Operational Audits, Incident Reviews)]
