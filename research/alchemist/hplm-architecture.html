[Table 1] Summary of Requirements by Production Logistics Scenario Analysis

Scenario Main Participants Key Functional Requirements Key Non-Functional Requirements HPLM-Specific Considerations
Line-feeding Operators, mobile robots, storage racks Timely material supply, route planning, station-level status monitoring Low latency, high reliability of inventory and location data Coordination between human decision and robot movement
Picking (order picking) Operators, vision systems, shelves Item identification, pick verification, error detection Privacy protection for operator images, robust tracking Modeling cognitive load, motion patterns, and task variability
Kitting Operators, workstations, container systems Kit completeness checks, sequence control, mis-pick prevention Traceability of components, consistency of kit configuration Representation of kit logic and human error patterns in the DT
AMR-based material transport AMRs, operators, traffic control systems Collision avoidance, rerouting, task assignment, zone access control Real-time position accuracy, network robustness Joint consideration of safety zones and human presence
Supervisory decision and control Supervisors, dashboards, AI modules KPI monitoring, exception handling, rescheduling, overrides Auditability of decisions, explainability Integration of AI suggestions with human-in-command mechanisms

[Table 2] Security/Ethics Requirements and Control Strategies

Category Requirement / Risk Control Strategy Related Standard / Reference
Device security Spoofing of mobile robot control commands Mutual authentication, signed commands, secure channels IEC 62443 (Zone/Conduit, Auth)
Network security Eavesdropping or tampering on sensor–DT communication Encrypted communication (TLS), VPN, segmented networks IEC 62443, IT security practices
Data protection Leakage of location / video data of operators Data minimization, anonymization/pseudonymization, strict access control GDPR
Transparency Lack of clarity on how operator data is used Consent Layer (opt-in/out, conditional use), transparent privacy policies GDPR (transparency, consent)
Explainability AI decisions not understandable to operators/supervisors Decision logging, explanation UI EU AI Act (high-risk AI)
Human oversight No effective override of AI/automation Human-in-command design, E-stop, approval workflow EU AI Act, safety engineering
Fairness Biased allocation of workload or tasks Fairness rules in scheduling, imbalance monitoring AI ethics guidelines
Traceability Difficulty reconstructing decision history Governance Module, audit trails EU AI Act (traceability)
Retention Excessive storage of personal data Retention/deletion policies, periodic review GDPR (storage limitations)
