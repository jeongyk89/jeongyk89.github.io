HPLM Trustworthy AI Checklist

Human-centric, ethical production logistics — aligned with EU Trustworthy AI, GDPR, IEC 62443, ISO 23247, and safety norms. The checklist below shows each criterion and the corresponding HPLM evidence (our Year 1 result).

1) Human Agency & Oversight Human-in-command Compliant

Question: Can a human stay in command and override autonomous actions?
HPLM Answer: Yes — Supervisor UI with explicit override; high-risk actions require human confirmation; decision log is visible in real time.

2) Technical Robustness & Safety ISO 13849 • IEC 61508 • IEC 62443 Compliant

Question: Are fail-safes, reliability and physical safety engineered into the system?
HPLM Answer: Yes — Safety PLCs and interlocks bound actuation; worst-case twin simulations executed; cybersecurity hardened per IEC 62443.

3) Privacy & Data Governance GDPR • Data minimization Compliant

Question: Are privacy, consent, and minimization ensured for personal data?
HPLM Answer: Yes — Data Classification & Consent Layer identifies personal streams, enforces minimization, consent/opt-out, and anonymization where applicable.

4) Transparency & Explainability XAI • Decision logs Compliant

Question: Can users understand why the AI acted or recommended an action?
HPLM Answer: Yes — Ethical Governance Module records rationale and inputs for every significant decision; explanations are accessible via the operator dashboard.

5) Fairness & Non-Discrimination Workload equity Compliant

Question: Are task allocations free of bias or undue burden?
HPLM Answer: Yes — Governance rules monitor workload distribution across workers and shifts; alerts raised on patterns that look unfair or exceed thresholds.

6) Human Well-being & Ergonomics Industry 5.0 Compliant

Question: Does optimization respect ergonomic limits and health?
HPLM Answer: Yes — Human task models in the twin; policy encodes ergonomic limits and break rules; schedules avoid fatigue-inducing plans.

7) Accountability & Audit Traceability Compliant

Question: Is there an end-to-end audit trail and assigned responsibility?
HPLM Answer: Yes — Complete decision audit trail; designated reviewer role (e.g., “Digital Twin Ethics Officer”); incident playbook and root-cause analysis process.

8) Cybersecurity & Data Integrity Defense-in-depth Compliant

Question: Are communications secured and the system resilient to cyber threats?
HPLM Answer: Yes — Encrypted and authenticated channels, least-privilege access, network zoning; OPC UA security profiles and access controls enforced.
Mapping to HPLM components (legend)
  • Consent & Privacy: Data Classification & Consent Layer
  • Transparency & Accountability: Ethical Governance Module
  • Safety & Robustness: Safety PLCs, interlocks, simulation tests
  • Human Agency: Supervisor UI with override & approvals
  • Cybersecurity: AuthN/AuthZ, encrypted transport, IEC 62443 zones
