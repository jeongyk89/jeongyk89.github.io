Connect the assets

ML2305 Lab 3 · interoperability station

Many suppliers, one plant view

Every asset in a factory comes from a different supplier. Each one sends its data its own way. Your job is to make them work together, three different ways, and to feel what each way costs.

Supplier

Everyone except the integrator

Pick an asset card in tab 1, define the JSON your asset sends, choose how often and press send. Stay true to the habits on your card.

Integrator

One person, rotate each round

Opens tab 2 on a laptop. Turns everything that arrives into one plant view with the same names, units and clock.

Observer

Optional, if you are five

Times how long integration takes, counts the rules the integrator writes and notes every misunderstanding.

Three rounds, about 20 minutes

Discuss
  1. The AMR stopped at 10:02:05 and the temperature peaked at 10:02:40, says the data. Did one cause the other?
  2. Would you order events by device time or by arrival time? What breaks in each case?
  3. One asset sends no time at all. What can you still do with it?
  4. How do real factories keep clocks in sync, and how exact must they be for picking, for robots, for energy billing?
