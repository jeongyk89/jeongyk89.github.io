Why a twin of one robot?
  1. Monitoring. See where the robot is and what it does from anywhere, together with other machines.
  2. Integration. The MiR brings a box, the UR picks it. One twin can coordinate both.
  3. People. An operator works next to the robot. What should the twin know about the person?

One robot alone rarely needs a twin. Think about which service would make a twin worth its cost in our lab.

  1. In Model, plan a motion. What can you test here without any robot?
